How it works

Your pipeline stays yours.

Your app keeps retrieval, generation, indexes and keys. TunaRAG owns the experiment around it.

01

Connect

Wrap your chain, graph or agent with an official adapter and a factory that builds it per candidate.

02

Define

Declare a search space, choose evaluators, and weight them into one objective across quality, cost and latency.

03

Optimize

Run the study with retries, caching and stop rules, then inspect ranked trials and export them to JSON or CSV.

Features

Structured studies, not eyeballed answers.

Every trial recorded, every failure isolated, every run reproducible.

One score for quality, cost and latency

Weight answer quality against provider cost and response time. Cost is marked exact, estimated or unavailable, never a fake $0.

Random or LLM-guided search

Seeded RandomSearch for reproducibility, or provider-neutral LLMSearch that proposes candidates from past trials.

Your metrics or RAGAS

Write a MetricEvaluator in one line of Python, or plug in RagasEvaluator for faithfulness and answer relevancy.

SQLite records and resume

SQLiteStore keeps every trial. An interrupted study picks up where it stopped instead of paying for the same calls twice.

Caching, retries and budgets

Retry policies, concurrency and a SQLiteCache, plus stop rules like MaxTrials, CostBudget and TimeBudget.

Bring data or synthesize it

Load records into EvaluationDataset or generate Q&A from your documents with SyntheticDatasetBuilder, with held-out splits.

Use cases

Questions TunaRAG answers.

“How many chunks should my retriever return?”

Search top_k, chunk size, reranking and prompt variants, and see how each one moves retrieval recall and answer quality.

Retrieval

“Is the cheaper model good enough?”

Compare models and generation settings in one weighted objective, so a small quality loss is set against real savings in cost and latency.

Cost & latency

“Did my change actually help?”

Score every configuration on the same dataset, keep the record in SQLite, and confirm the winner on held-out data.

quickstart.pyPython
try:
    result = optimizer.optimize(dataset, study_name="my-rag")
    print(result.best_candidate.parameters)

    # rank by any single metric
    result.metric_leaders("retrieval_recall")

    # export for your notebook or dashboard
    result.write_json("artifacts/result.json")
    result.write_csv("artifacts/trials.csv")
finally:
    optimizer.close()

FAQ

Good questions.

Do I have to rebuild my RAG pipeline?

No. You keep your chain, graph or agent. TunaRAG calls a factory you provide for each candidate configuration and reads the answers and usage it returns.

Which frameworks are supported?

LangChain, LangGraph and Google ADK 2.x through official adapters. Each is an optional extra, and Google ADK needs Python 3.10 or newer.

Will it spend money on API calls?

Only the calls your pipeline and evaluators make. The offline quickstart uses fake models and needs no keys, and budgets such as CostBudget stop a study early.

What happens if a study is interrupted?

With SQLiteStore, every trial is persisted, so you can resume the study instead of starting over. Cached evaluations are reused.

How accurate is the cost tracking?

Usage reports say whether cost is exact, estimated from a price table, or unavailable. An unknown cost is never shown as an exact $0.
